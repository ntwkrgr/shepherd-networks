## 1. SERVICES

Provider offers a range of professional network consulting and managed services, including but not limited to:
- Network monitoring and alerting
- DNS security management
- Equipment installation and configuration
- Network design (wireless, new construction, remodel)
- Troubleshooting and optimization
- Security camera system design and installation

Individual service offerings are described in separate Service Agreements, Statements of Work (SOW), or quotes, each of which is incorporated by reference into this MSA.

### 1.1 Service-Specific Terms
Where a specific Service Agreement conflicts with this MSA, the Service Agreement controls for that particular service. Otherwise, this MSA governs all services provided by Provider to Client.

## 2. TERM & TERMINATION

### 2.1 Term
This MSA is effective on the Effective Date above and continues indefinitely unless terminated by either party.

### 2.2 Termination
- **Month-to-Month Services:** Either party may terminate any recurring service with **30 days written notice** via email.
- **Project-Based Services:** Existing projects continue through completion unless terminated for cause. Termination requires **written notice** and is subject to pro-rata billing.
- **For Cause:** Either party may terminate immediately if the other materially breaches and fails to cure within **15 days** of written notice.

### 2.3 Effects of Termination
Upon termination:
- Client remains responsible for fees accrued through the termination date
- Provider will remove monitoring configurations, restore default DNS settings, or perform other wind-down activities as appropriate
- Client data and network credentials remain the Client's property

## 3. FEES, BILLING & PAYMENT

### 3.1 Pricing
Pricing for services is specified in:
- Individual Service Agreements
- Statements of Work (SOW)
- Quotes provided by Provider

Unless otherwise stated, pricing is in USD and excludes applicable sales tax.

### 3.2 Billing
- **Recurring Services:** Billed monthly on the same date each month, in advance
- **Hourly Services:** Billed upon completion of work
- **Project Services:** Billed per the payment schedule in the SOW or quote

### 3.3 Payment Terms
- Payment is due within **30 days** of invoice (or as specified in the applicable service agreement)
- Accepted payment methods: credit/debit card, check, or other arrangement agreed in writing
- Invoices are sent via email to the Client contact address provided

### 3.4 Late Payment
If payment is not received by the due date:
- A late fee of **1.5% per month** (or the maximum allowed by Texas law) will accrue
- Provider may suspend services without further notice until the account is current
- Client is responsible for any collection costs (legal fees, court costs, etc.)

### 3.5 Recurring Service Cancellation
To cancel a recurring service:
- Send written cancellation notice **30 days** before the next billing cycle
- Cancellation takes effect on the date specified in the notice
- No refunds are provided for partial months

### 10.1 Informal Resolution

### 10.2 Mediation

### 10.3 Arbitration
**Any dispute that cannot be resolved through negotiation or mediation shall be resolved by binding arbitration** under the American Arbitration Association (AAA) Commercial Arbitration Rules. The arbitration shall:
- Take place in San Angelo, Texas
- Be conducted by a single arbitrator with relevant experience
- Follow the AAA rules and procedures
- Result in a final, binding award

**Exception:** Either party may seek injunctive relief in a court of competent jurisdiction to prevent irreparable harm.

This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
