## Service Description

The **Network Monitoring** service provides continuous, around-the-clock monitoring of Client's network infrastructure with proactive alerting and regular health reporting.

### Included Services

- 24/7 uptime and availability monitoring for routers, switches, and access points
- Wireless performance metrics: signal strength, client load, channel utilization
- Proactive alerting when devices go offline or performance degrades
- Monthly summary report of network health and trends
- Compatible with most platforms — hardware compatibility confirmed during initial setup
