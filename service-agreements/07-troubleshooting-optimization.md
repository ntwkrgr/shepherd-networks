## What to Expect

1. Provider arrives on-site and assesses the current state of the network
2. Diagnostics are performed: speed tests, interference analysis, configuration review
3. Root causes are identified and fixes are implemented on the spot where possible
4. A summary of findings and recommendations is provided, including any follow-up items (e.g., equipment upgrades, cabling changes)
