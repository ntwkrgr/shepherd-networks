## Service Description

Provider will deliver a comprehensive network design for Client's new construction or remodel project, including structured cabling layout, equipment planning, and coordination with builders and electricians.

### Included Deliverables

- Structured cabling layout and specifications
- Ethernet drop placement for offices, living areas, and common spaces
- Network closet and equipment rack planning
- Coordination with builders and electricians
- Low-voltage wiring recommendations
- Documentation and labeling plans
- Equipment list with pricing and sourcing guidance
