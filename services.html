Our Services

🏗️ New Construction Network Design

Building a new home or commercial space is the perfect time to plan your network infrastructure. We work with you and your builder to design a structured cabling system that meets your needs now and in the future.

  • Structured cabling layout and specifications
  • Ethernet drop placement for offices, living areas, and common spaces
  • Network closet and equipment rack planning
  • Coordination with builders and electricians
  • Low-voltage wiring recommendations
  • Documentation and labeling plans

A well-planned network starts at the blueprint stage. We help ensure your new construction is wired for reliable, high-performance connectivity from day one.

📶 Wireless Network Design

Eliminate dead spots and enjoy fast, reliable Wi-Fi in every corner of your home or business. We design wireless networks that deliver consistent coverage and performance.

  • Site surveys and coverage analysis
  • Access point placement and quantity recommendations
  • Equipment selection guidance (routers, access points, mesh systems)
  • Guest network and VLAN configuration planning
  • Outdoor and large-area wireless coverage
  • Integration with wired network infrastructure

Whether you need whole-home coverage or a multi-building commercial deployment, we design wireless networks that just work.

🔧 Optimization & Troubleshooting

Is your network slow, unreliable, or just not working the way it should? We diagnose issues and optimize your existing setup for peak performance.

  • Network performance assessment and speed testing
  • Wi-Fi interference analysis and channel optimization
  • Router and switch configuration review
  • Bandwidth management and Quality of Service (QoS) tuning
  • Connectivity issue diagnosis and resolution
  • Security audit and firmware update review

We identify the root cause of your network problems and provide practical solutions to get you back up and running at full speed.

📷 Security Camera System Consulting & Design

Protect your property with a professionally designed security camera system. We help you plan the right system for your needs, from camera selection to placement and recording setup.

  • Site assessment and camera placement planning
  • IP camera system design (PoE and wireless options)
  • NVR/DVR selection and storage planning
  • Remote viewing and mobile app setup guidance
  • Integration with existing network infrastructure
  • Indoor and outdoor coverage recommendations

From single-camera setups to comprehensive multi-camera systems, we design solutions that give you peace of mind and clear visibility of your property.
