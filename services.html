DNS Security

Your network's first line of defense. DNS-level filtering blocks malicious websites, phishing attempts, and known bad actors before a connection is ever made — protecting every device on your network automatically.

  • Blocks malicious domains, phishing sites, and malware delivery infrastructure
  • Custom filtering rules tailored to your business
  • No software to install on individual devices — protects everything on the network
  • Delivered via NextDNS (compatible with most hardware) or UniFi Shield (requires UniFi Dream Machine or Dream Router)
  • We handle setup, configuration, and ongoing management

$25/month. Available as a standalone service or as part of the Network Performance & Security Bundle.
