# Security Camera System Consulting & Design

**Service Type:** Hourly (design) + Flat Rate (installation)
**Design Price:** $95/hr (2-hour minimum)
**Installation Price:** $100 flat per camera
**Status:** Active offering

---

## What's Included

### Design & Consulting ($95/hr)
- Site assessment and camera placement planning
- IP camera system design (PoE and wireless options)
- NVR/DVR selection and storage planning
- Remote viewing and mobile app setup guidance
- Integration with existing network infrastructure
- Indoor and outdoor coverage recommendations

### Installation ($100/camera)
- Physical mounting of cameras
- Cabling (minor runs included; major runs quoted separately)
- Configuration of recording, motion detection, and alerts
- Integration into camera management system
- Remote viewing setup and walkthrough

## Ideal For

- Homeowners wanting to secure their property with a professionally designed camera system
- Businesses needing comprehensive surveillance coverage
- Anyone who wants cameras that actually work reliably and integrate with their network

## How It Works

1. Free initial consultation to discuss needs and goals
2. Site assessment: I walk the property and recommend camera types, placement, and quantities
3. Equipment recommendations with pricing and sourcing guidance
4. Client purchases equipment
5. I install, configure, and test the full system
6. Walkthrough: how to view live feeds, review recordings, and manage the system
