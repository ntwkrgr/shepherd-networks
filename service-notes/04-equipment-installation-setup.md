# Equipment Installation & Setup

**Service Type:** On-Site, Flat Rate
**Status:** Active offering

---

## Pricing

| Item | Rate | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| Managed Switch Installation & Setup | $150 flat | Physical mount, cabling, VLAN configuration, network integration |
| Access Point Installation & Setup | $100 flat per AP | Mount, cable, configure SSID and wireless settings |
| IP Camera Installation & Setup | $100 flat per camera | Mount, cable, configure recording and remote viewing |

**Equipment costs are never included in installation rates.** Equipment is purchased by the client, with sourcing guidance provided.

Minor cabling is included. Significant cabling runs are subcontracted and quoted separately.

## Ideal For

- Clients who have purchased (or plan to purchase) network equipment and want it installed correctly
- Businesses upgrading switches, adding access points, or deploying cameras
- Homeowners adding PoE cameras or expanding wireless coverage

## How It Works

1. Client purchases equipment (I can provide recommendations and sourcing guidance)
2. I arrive on-site and perform physical installation (mounting, cabling)
3. Full configuration: VLANs, SSIDs, firmware updates, network integration
4. Testing and verification that everything works as expected
5. Brief walkthrough with the client on how to use/manage the new equipment
