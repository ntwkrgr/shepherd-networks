# New Construction & Remodel Network Design

**Service Type:** Project Work, Flat Rate
**Price:** $500–$1,500 (scope-dependent)
**Project Management:** 15–20% of total project cost (for multi-phase or subcontracted work)
**Status:** Active offering

---

## What's Included

- Structured cabling layout and specifications
- Ethernet drop placement for offices, living areas, and common spaces
- Network closet and equipment rack planning
- Coordination with builders and electricians
- Low-voltage wiring recommendations
- Documentation and labeling plans
- Equipment list with pricing and sourcing guidance

## Ideal For

- Homeowners building a new home or doing a significant remodel
- Businesses building out a new office, retail, or commercial space
- Anyone who wants network infrastructure designed before the walls go up

## How It Works

1. Free initial consultation to understand the project scope and client needs
2. I review floor plans and coordinate with the builder/electrician
3. Deliverable: a complete network design document with cable routing, drop locations, equipment list, and specifications
4. Optional project management: I coordinate subcontractors and track milestones throughout the build
