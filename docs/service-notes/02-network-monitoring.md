# Network Monitoring

**Service Type:** Recurring Monthly
**Price:** $75/month
**Status:** Active offering

---

## What's Included

- 24/7 uptime and availability monitoring for routers, switches, and access points
- Wireless performance metrics: signal strength, client load, channel utilization
- Proactive alerting when devices go offline or performance degrades
- Monthly summary report of network health and trends
- Compatible with most platforms — ask about specific hardware compatibility

## Ideal For

- Businesses that rely on consistent network uptime for operations
- Multi-AP or multi-site environments where manual monitoring isn't practical
- Clients who want to know about problems before their customers or employees notice

## How It Works

1. I configure monitoring agents/integrations on the client's network equipment
2. Monitoring runs continuously; alerts are generated for outages, degraded performance, or unusual patterns
3. Alerts are reviewed and triaged — client is contacted for actionable issues
4. A monthly health summary is provided with trends and recommendations

## Commitment

Month-to-month or annual billing available. No long-term contract required.
