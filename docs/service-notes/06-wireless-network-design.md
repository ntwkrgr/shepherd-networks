## What's Included

- Site surveys and coverage analysis
- Access point placement and quantity recommendations
- Equipment selection guidance (routers, access points, mesh systems)
- Guest network and VLAN configuration planning
- Outdoor and large-area wireless coverage design
- Integration with wired network infrastructure

## Ideal For

- Homes or businesses with dead spots, slow Wi-Fi, or unreliable connections
- Multi-building campuses or large properties needing outdoor coverage
- Businesses that need guest networks, VLANs, or segmented wireless access
- Anyone planning a wireless upgrade or expansion

## How It Works

1. Site survey or coverage analysis to assess current conditions
2. I recommend access point placement, quantity, and equipment
3. Design document with configuration recommendations
4. Optional: I can handle the installation as well (see Equipment Installation & Setup)
