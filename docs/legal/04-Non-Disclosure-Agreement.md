## 1. PURPOSE

The Parties acknowledge that during the course of business discussions and service delivery, one or both Parties may have access to confidential or proprietary information. This NDA establishes terms for protecting such information.

---

## 2. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

### 2.1 Provider's Confidential Information
Information disclosed by Provider to Client that is proprietary or confidential, including:
- Methodologies, processes, and proprietary tools
- Pricing and billing practices
- Client lists and references (if disclosed)
- Internal business practices and strategies
- Any information marked "Confidential" or identified as proprietary

### 2.2 Client's Confidential Information
Information disclosed by Client to Provider that is proprietary or confidential, including:
- Network configuration and topology details
- IP addresses, domain names, and system architecture
- Access credentials, passwords, and authentication information
- Security policies, procedures, and vulnerabilities
- Business processes, operations, and sensitive data
- Financial information and customer data
- Any information marked "Confidential" or identified as proprietary

### 2.3 Exclusions from Confidential Information
Confidential Information does **not** include information that:

1. **Public Domain** — Was publicly available at the time of disclosure or becomes public through no breach by the Receiving Party
2. **Prior Knowledge** — Was rightfully known to the Receiving Party before disclosure (as evidenced by written records)
3. **Third-Party Source** — Was rightfully obtained from a third party without breach of confidentiality obligations
4. **Independent Development** — Was independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to the Confidential Information
5. **Required Disclosure** — Must be disclosed under law, court order, or regulatory requirement (with notice as described in Section 5)

---

## 3. OBLIGATIONS OF RECEIVING PARTY

### 3.1 Confidentiality
The Receiving Party agrees to:
- Maintain strict confidentiality of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information
- Use Confidential Information **only** for the purpose of this business relationship (service delivery, evaluation, or negotiation)
- Not disclose Confidential Information to third parties without the Disclosing Party's written consent
- Implement reasonable measures to protect Confidential Information from unauthorized access or disclosure

### 3.2 Limited Disclosure
The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to:
- Employees, contractors, and advisors who **need to know** the information to perform their duties
- Only if these individuals are bound by written confidentiality obligations at least as restrictive as this NDA

### 3.3 Standard of Care
The Receiving Party agrees to protect Confidential Information using the **same degree of care** it uses to protect its own confidential information, but **no less than reasonable care**.

---

## 4. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION OF INFORMATION

Upon request by the Disclosing Party, or upon termination of this business relationship, the Receiving Party will:
- Return or securely destroy all Confidential Information in its possession
- Certify in writing that destruction has been completed
- Retain only one archival copy for legal compliance purposes (if permitted by law)

**Exception:** Confidential Information may be retained if legally required to be preserved (e.g., for regulatory compliance or litigation). Such retained information remains subject to confidentiality obligations.

---

## 5. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

### 5.1 Legal Obligation
If the Receiving Party is legally compelled to disclose Confidential Information (by court order, subpoena, regulatory agency, or government demand), the Receiving Party will:
1. **Promptly notify** the Disclosing Party in writing (unless legally prohibited)
2. **Provide the Disclosing Party** with a copy of the legal demand
3. **Cooperate** with the Disclosing Party's efforts to obtain a protective order
4. **Disclose only the minimum information** legally required
5. **Request confidential treatment** of the disclosed information from the requesting authority

### 5.2 No Breach
Compelled disclosure, if notice is provided and cooperation is offered, does not constitute a breach of this NDA.

---

## 6. PERMITTED DISCLOSURES

### 6.1 Provider May Disclose (With Consent or Anonymization)
Provider may use Client's information for:
- **Portfolio/Case Studies** — Anonymized descriptions of projects (without Client name, identifying details, or specific configurations) for marketing and professional purposes
- **References** — If Client agrees, identify Client as a reference
- **Industry Examples** — Redacted, anonymized examples in presentations or publications

### 6.2 Client Limitations
Client agrees that it will not disclose Provider's methodologies, pricing, or proprietary processes to competing vendors without Provider's written consent.

---

## 7. NO LICENSE OR OBLIGATION

### 7.1 No License Granted
Disclosure of Confidential Information does **not** grant any license, rights, or ownership to the Receiving Party. All intellectual property rights remain with the Disclosing Party.

### 7.2 No Obligation to Purchase
Disclosure does not obligate either Party to:
- Purchase services or products
- Enter into a contract
- Disclose additional information
- Continue negotiations

### 7.3 No Warranty
Confidential Information is provided "as-is" without warranty of accuracy, completeness, or fitness. The Disclosing Party does not warrant that Confidential Information is correct or that use will not infringe third-party rights.

---

## 8. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION SECURITY

### 8.1 Provider's Security Practices
Provider will implement reasonable security measures to protect Client's Confidential Information:
- Encryption for sensitive data
- Limited access on a need-to-know basis
- Secure deletion practices
- Regular security reviews

### 8.2 Client Responsibility
Client is responsible for:
- Maintaining strong credentials and access controls
- Changing passwords after Provider's work is complete
- Rotating encryption keys or re-securing systems as needed
- Implementing additional security measures appropriate to Client's risk profile

---

## 9. DURATION

### 9.1 Confidentiality Period
Obligations under this NDA apply:
- **During the business relationship** — As long as services are being provided
- **After termination** — For **five (5) years** after the termination date, or as long as the information remains confidential (whichever is longer)

### 9.2 Survival
The confidentiality obligations survive termination of any service agreement.

---

## 10. REMEDIES

### 10.1 Inadequacy of Damages
Both Parties acknowledge that breach of this NDA could cause irreparable harm that cannot be adequately remedied by monetary damages alone.

### 10.2 Injunctive Relief
In addition to any other remedies available at law or equity, the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief (restraining order, specific performance) to prevent breach or continued breach of this NDA.

### 10.3 Legal Remedies
Each Party retains all rights and remedies available under applicable law, including:
- Damages (actual, consequential, punitive)
- Legal fees and court costs
- Specific performance
